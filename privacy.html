Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy covers the SharifTech publishing tool, a private app used only by the owner of the SharifTech YouTube channel to upload that channel's videos and read that channel's own statistics.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to upload videos to the SharifTech channel, read the privacy status of its own videos, and read its own aggregate video statistics (for example views, average percentage viewed, likes, shares and subscribers gained).

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process personal information about viewers or anyone else.
  • It does not read comments, messages or any other account's data.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data, and does not use it for advertising.

How data is used and stored

Aggregate statistics are used only to decide how future SharifTech videos are made (for example, which opening or length works better). They are stored on the owner's own computer and server and kept for as long as the channel runs. Sign-in tokens are stored with owner-only file permissions and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
